# ADR NNNN: Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or roles

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints, and what makes it hard. State facts, not
opinions. Name the alternatives you considered.

## Decision

The decision, in one or two sentences, in the present tense: "We will ...".

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder, or is now ruled out.
- What follow-up work the decision creates.
